# Buddy Design Direction

Buddy uses an Impeccable-style product UI: restrained, specific, and useful on
the first screen.

## Rules

- Map/search is the primary surface; avoid landing-page or chatbot framing.
- Use compact mission-control sections: status, timeline, evidence, call, report.
- Keep cards shallow and purposeful. Do not nest cards.
- Avoid purple gradients, glassmorphism, decorative blobs, and oversized hero type.
- Show timestamps, confidence, expiry, source, call summary, and full transcript.
- Use local render payloads instead of paid OpenUI.
- Keep access language careful: confirmed, likely, unknown, risky. Never say
  "definitely accessible."
- Default call safety must stay visible: real venue calls require explicit opt-in.
